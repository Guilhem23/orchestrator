# Open Decisions for Future Orchestrator Evolution

These are not settled requirements. They are questions the future runtime/method may need to resolve.

## O-001 — How much product judgment should be automated?
Define the safe boundary between proactive PO/SM behavior and human product authority.

## O-002 — Product objective representation
Determine whether Objectives need explicit outcome metrics beyond acceptance predicates.

## O-003 — Question escalation policy
Define a small, measurable question budget and escalation classes so the orchestrator remains autonomous without becoming reckless.

## O-004 — Strategic architecture challenge
Determine whether the orchestrator should have a dedicated non-authoritative “architect/advisor” worker for challenging direction, distinct from the adversarial acceptance reviewer.

## O-005 — Learning promotion
Define when repeated learnings can become durable project guidance and what human/control-plane approval is required.
